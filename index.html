About

Welcome to Strata

Strata is a web app for visualising the history of websites as recorded by web archives. Using data gathered by Pluto, it draws every URL as a layer on a shared timeline, from its first archived capture to its last, revealing when pages appeared, how long they lasted and what survives today. Zoom from decades down to a single capture and open it as it was, in the archive that holds it.

Reading the timeline

  • A URL. Line brightness corresponds to capture frequency
  • A capture. Zoom in and click one to open it
  • Still live on the web today

Use your own data

  1. Run Pluto on the URLs you want to explore
  2. Click Upload Data and choose its results folder, or drag it onto the page
