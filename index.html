About

Welcome to Strata

The web is ephemeral: webpages change, are superseded and vanish. Web archives have kept copies since 1996, but their records are scattered across institutions and frequently read one webpage at a time.

Strata brings these records together, laying down the archived history of each URL as a layer, or stratum, on a single timeline. Sort the layers, switch to charts of survival and growth, filter by web archive or type of URL, or search for a single address.

The demo explores The University of Edinburgh's web estate (ed.ac.uk): 8,706 URLs from 14 web archives, gathered in August 2026. Read more
